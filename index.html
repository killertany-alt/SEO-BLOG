Digital marketing for beginners starts with understanding how people find things online. The AI Marketing Playbook gives you ten long, practical guides on SEO basics, keyword research, AI marketing tools, blogging, social media and hosting your own website, all written in simple language with step-by-step answers.

You will learn how search engines work, how to research keywords, how to optimize every page, how to use AI tools safely and how to publish a free website that Google can find. Start with what SEO is if you are new.
