Stock market basics: your questions answered

What is the best way to start learning the stock market?

The best way to start is to learn how shares, exchanges and prices work, then open a demat account and invest a small amount. Begin with our guide to how the stock market works, then follow the guides in order.

How much money do you need to start investing in stocks?

You can start with a few hundred rupees. Many brokers and mutual funds allow small purchases, and a monthly plan starts from as little as ₹100 to ₹500. See SIP vs lump sum investing to choose how to begin.

Is the stock market safe for beginners?

The stock market is not risk free, and you can lose money. Beginners reduce risk by investing small amounts, spreading money across companies and using a stop-loss, which we explain in risk management in stock trading.
